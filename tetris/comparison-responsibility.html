Who knows what? Tetris 001 → 004

A hypothesis-first review using the validator lens: cohesive responsibilities, behavior beside its knowledge, narrow collaborations, and the simplest design needed today.

Supported—with one open seam 004 improves knowledge placement and boundary collaboration. Its mutable game state remains broadly exposed.

Hypothesis

If the lens shaped 004, presentation should stop reconstructing game state; gravity rules should live with the game rather than the terminal; and terminal work should be split into collaborators with narrow messages. Expect visibleCells(), update(deltaMs), and a renderer/session boundary—not extra classes for their own sake.

Small next improvement

Add intention-revealing scenario methods or a narrow state/snapshot builder for tests, then stop exposing writable active, board, and bag. Do this only where it removes real test setup coupling; the lens explicitly rejects speculative class-making.

Interpretation

The architectural claim is stronger than “004 has more files/tests.” One validator finding names misplaced knowledge, the repair moves it to the owner, and a boundary test prevents regression. That is responsibility-driven change with traceable evidence.

Conclusion. The hypothesis holds at two important seams: presentation no longer understands piece geometry, and the terminal no longer owns gravity policy. 004 is more intention-revealing and change-local than 001, while its public mutable state remains the clearest design debt.
