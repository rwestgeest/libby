Testability lens · evidence report

Did the third Tetris become more testable than the first?

A hypothesis-led comparison of tetris-003 and tetris-001, built from the same seed. Evidence comes from plans, production code, tests, and fresh test/coverage runs.

Partly supported

003 has better-controlled boundaries: rendering is separate and the session accepts clocks, I/O, process events, keyboard setup, and drawing as dependencies. But it is not demonstrably better tested: 001 has more tests and slightly higher coverage. With no validator findings recorded in 003’s final plan, the architectural difference cannot be causally credited to validation.

Hypothesis

If the testability lens affected the build, 003 should isolate pure game/rendering logic from terminal side effects, replace real time and global process state with controllable collaborators, and test behavior deterministically. It need not simply have “more tests”; it should make difficult behavior cheaper and less flaky to exercise.
