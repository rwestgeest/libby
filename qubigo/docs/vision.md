# Qubigo Vision

## Chess meets 3D tic-tac-toe.

Qubigo is a deceptively simple 3-in-a-row strategy game played on a 3×3×3 board.

Same goal as tic-tac-toe — three in a row. Lines run across planes, through layers, and diagonally through space. With 27 positions, three shapes, three colours, and four piece patterns, every move is a choice that shapes the board.

The digital edition brings that experience to web and mobile — an on-ramp to the physical board game. Its job is not to replace the real thing, but to let players fall in love with Qubigo online and then **buy the physical game**.

We ship a single, focused experience:

- **Play online** against friends, or pass-and-play on the same device.
- **Explore in 3D** — the full Three.js board you can orbit, zoom, and inspect.
- **Learn in minutes** — the rules fit on a napkin; the strategy takes a lifetime.
- **Stay connected** — the app is a direct channel to the Qubigo community: news, events, and a path to the physical game.

Everything in this codebase serves one purpose: to turn a curious player into a Qubigo fan — and eventually, a Qubigo owner.
